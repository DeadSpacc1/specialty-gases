about company

Powering Industries with Precision

Speciality Group is an Indian company that provides cryogenic solutions, industrial and speciality gases, ammonia products, technical coatings, and infection testing kits. Based in Ahmedabad, the company has over 100 years of combined experience. It includes Speciality Gases Pvt. Ltd., known for its high-quality gases, and Speciality Ammonia LLP, which focuses on delivering reliable ammonia products.

They also have Speciality Ventures, which develops safer, non-toxic coatings, including antimicrobial solutions used during the COVID-19 pandemic. Speciality Group is known for its focus on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, making it a trusted name across different industries.
